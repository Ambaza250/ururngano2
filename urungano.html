Ubuzima bwo mu mutwe

Soma amasomo yoroshye ku marangamutima no ku buzima bwo mu mutwe. Menya ibimenyetso byakwereka ko wowe cyangwa undi muntu akeneye ubufasha. Subiza ibibazo by’ibanga bigufasha kureba uko umaze iminsi wiyumva.
