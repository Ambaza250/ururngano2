Urungano • Ubuzima bw’imyororokere

Murakaza neza ku makuru yo kuboneza urubyaro

Menya uburyo bwo kuboneza urubyaro kandi ugishe inama umukozi w’ubuzima ku buryo bukubereye.

Uburyo bwo kuboneza urubyaro

Uburyo buratandukana mu mikoreshereze no mu mikorere; umukozi w’ubuzima wabihuguriwe yagufasha guhitamo neza.

Inshinge zo kuboneza urubyaro

Urushinge rutangwa buri kwezi 1 cyangwa 3 bitewe n’ubwoko bwarwo.

Ibinini by’ihutirwa

Bikoreshwa vuba bishoboka nyuma y’imibonano idakingiye; bigenewe ibihe byihutirwa, si uburyo busanzwe.

Ibinini bya buri munsi

Binyobwa buri munsi; umukozi w’ubuzima yagusobanurira uko utangira n’icyo wakora wibagiwe ikinini.

Agakingirizo

Koresha agakingirizo buri gihe ukoze imibonano mpuzabitsina; gafasha no kugabanya ibyago byo kwandura indwara zandurira mu mibonano.

Uburyo buramba

Utubuto dushyirwa mu kuboko n’udupira dushyirwa muri nyababyeyi birinda gusama igihe kirekire, bigashyirwamo n’umukozi wabyigiye.
